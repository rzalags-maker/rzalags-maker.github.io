# Gulf of Riga navigation land

Source: [Natural Earth 1:10m physical land, version 5.1.1](https://naturalearth.s3.amazonaws.com/10m_physical/ne_10m_land.zip), public domain. Clipped to 21.7–25.1° E and 56.6–58.6° N with GDAL `ogr2ogr -clipsrc 21.7 56.6 25.1 58.6 -lco COORDINATE_PRECISION=6`.

This regional dataset is for game navigation and has generalized coastlines. Existing Latvia land and inland water data are retained. Outside the clip, surface classification remains unknown.
